# Data sources and licences

The **code** is MIT ([LICENSE](LICENSE)). The **data** shown by Semaphore and republished in `docs/data/*.json` comes from the
sources below and stays under their licences. If you reuse the JSON files, follow those terms (attribution, and share-alike for ODbL).
Where a source states no explicit licence we link to it and republish only the minimum (values, titles and links).
